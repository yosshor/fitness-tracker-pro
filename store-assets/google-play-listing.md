## Full Description (4000 chars max)

```
Train smarter with Fitness Tracker Pro — the AI-powered gym companion built for lifters who want more than a basic workout logger.

Whether you're tracking your bench press PR, generating a new routine, or chatting with an AI coach about nutrition — this app has everything you need to level up your training.

SMART WORKOUT LOGGING
Log every set, rep, and weight with an intuitive workout tracker. Track RPE intensity for each session. Browse a built-in exercise library with muscle group filtering. See your personal bests and exercise history at a glance. Add session notes to remember what worked.

AI WORKOUT GENERATOR
Generate custom workout routines powered by artificial intelligence. Select your training split — Chest & Biceps, Back & Arms, Legs, or Full Body. Adjust volume per muscle group. Get progressive overload suggestions based on your actual training history. Never wonder "what should I do today?" again.

ANALYTICS & PROGRESS CHARTS
Visualize your gains with interactive weight progression charts. Track intensity vs. volume over time. Filter by 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, or all time. Monitor your best lift, average RPE, and total lifetime volume. Data-driven training leads to better results.

PROGRESS PHOTOS
Document your body transformation with progress photos. Upload from gallery or capture directly in-app. Organize chronologically to see how far you've come. Visual tracking keeps you motivated when the scale doesn't move.

AI FITNESS COACH
Chat with an AI-powered fitness coach anytime. Get advice on sports nutrition, meal plans, exercise technique, muscle building, fat loss, workout programming, and recovery. Like having a personal trainer in your pocket.

WORKOUT HISTORY
Browse all your past sessions with search and filtering. Filter by muscle group — chest, back, legs, shoulders, arms. Repeat any previous workout with one tap. Track your consistency and never lose your training data.

TRAINING SPLITS & CUSTOMIZATION
Configure your preferred training split and volume level. Choose from Split A (Chest & Biceps), Split B (Back & Arms), Split C (Legs), or Full Body. Set exercises per muscle group to match your training style.

BUILT FOR SERIOUS LIFTERS
- Clean dark theme designed for the gym
- Fast, distraction-free workout logging
- Works offline for logging sessions
- Export your data anytime as JSON
- Bilingual support: English and Hebrew
- Sign in securely with email or Google
- Your data is encrypted and protected

Fitness Tracker Pro is the gym log and workout planner that adapts to you. Whether you're a beginner starting your first split or an experienced lifter tracking progressive overload — this app grows with you.

Download now and start training smarter.
```
